# SEO Keyword Strategy

No search-volume, ranking, or competitor keyword dataset was available, so this is an intent-based hypothesis to validate in Google Search Console, Keyword Planner, and paid/organic query data.

| Intent | Cluster | Primary page | Supporting pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transactional | custom t-shirts, design your own t-shirt, personalized t-shirt | `/custom-t-shirts` | `/catalog`, relevant blog guides |
| Transactional | custom hoodies, personalized hoodie, design your own hoodie | `/custom-hoodies` | `/catalog`, hoodie design article |
| Transactional | personalized mugs, custom photo mug, custom mug online | `/custom-mugs` | `/catalog`, gift-oriented future content only if capability is verified |
| Transactional | custom tote bags, personalized tote bag | `/custom-tote-bags` | `/catalog` |
| Commercial | personalized gifts, custom clothing gifts, custom products for gifts | Homepage and category pages | FAQ, product pages |
| Commercial | AI-generated t-shirt design, AI clothing design | Homepage/how-it-works | AI design guide, catalog |
| Informational | how to design a custom t-shirt, how print on demand works | Existing blog pages | Category CTA and internal links |
| B2B secondary | print-on-demand products, launch a clothing brand, sell custom products online | Existing B2B pages/blog | Pricing, how-it-works, signup |

## Rules

- Use one primary intent per page; do not create near-identical doorway pages.
- Keep product pages focused on actual product names, visible variants, price, and customization path.
- Add new gift pages only when the product and fulfillment flow genuinely support the use case.
- Validate language and country modifiers from real queries before creating localized pages.
